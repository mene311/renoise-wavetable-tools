Wavetables into Renoise instruments

Drop wavetables, get .xrni files that play on Renoise's own sampler: one sample slot per frame, a gate LFO per frame, one macro walking the table. Everything happens in this page.

Cycle length sets register and headroom: 169 samples sits at C-4 with 84 harmonics, 1070 at 41 Hz with 535. Spectral frame selection keeps the frames that differ most, which sweeps better than evenly spaced ones.

What you get

An .xrni with the frames stacked as sample slots, one gate LFO per slot whose triangle envelopes add up to 1.0, and macro 1 named WT Position walking the gate positions. Drop it in User Library/Instruments/, hold a note and turn the macro.

Nothing gets uploaded: your browser reads the files and assembles the zip itself.
